Our Mission Statement

The Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard Foundation envisions a world where the streets that bear the great name of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. are beacons of hope for other communities to follow.

Our charity emulates the values of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.—including equality, liberty, nonviolence, education, love, selflessness, leadership, and hope—in everything we do.

We strive for a better world that includes every member of society, regardless of strength, wealth, race, or religion—a world where people are judged by the content of their character.

Donations to our charity will be allocated responsibly to benefit communities as effectively as possible.

Our Goals

  • Build strong communities
  • Increase poverty awareness
  • Reduce homelessness
  • Create jobs
  • Fight hunger and food insecurity
  • Help veterans

The Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard Foundation connects, serves, and empowers citizens in need by providing free programs and services that address their needs and fill gaps in government care.

Our Programs

  • Food distribution
  • Housing placement
  • Community activities
  • Free educational classes

Every donation to the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard Foundation directly supports one of our programs.
